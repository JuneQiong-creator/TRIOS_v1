# Scope and known limitations

1. This is a scoped public reproduction release, not a demonstrated full re-execution of all paper experiments. Figure replay, stored-result checking and computation from data are separate levels. No complete simulation bank, 81-method benchmark, LOO bank or bootstrap was rerun for release.
2. The small HB example reloads frozen fit states and evaluates one existing regimen through the original stage/CRS/AB path; it does not refit or reproduce all benchmark comparisons. A synthetic CCLE fixed-score example exercises classifier-only folds and ORIGINAL5 aggregation; it does not reselect domains or demonstrate all dataset rankings.
3. Actual legacy CCLE raw files and raw cohort derivatives are omitted because exact-file mirror permission was not confirmed. Frozen full score/label/fold banks needed for complete current CCLE incremental execution are not a verified portable public input set. Study-derived aggregate results and figure tables are included; official source acquisition and additional packaging are required for complete recomputation.
4. Available generator/producer scripts do not imply all their historical inputs are packaged. Original binary fit-state/checkpoint banks, external instructions, historical dependency snapshots and large archives are omitted. No unavailable input is replaced with another result version.
5. Historical HB and simulation AB binary64 objective comparisons can differ from mathematical exact-arithmetic lexmin on the artificial equal-objective fixture. The later guarded exact-dyadic shortlist helper remains attached only to the runs that used it; the shortlist has no general proven global-optimum inclusion guarantee. Independent stored-score oracle audits do not certify ideal real-valued CRS.
